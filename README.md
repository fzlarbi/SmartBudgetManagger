# SmartBudgetManagger
A project for managing Budget

**Track your monthly income and expenses in Excel, see your balance at a glance, and get a warning when you go over budget.**

*Prepared by YourName – Group | ESI-SBA, 2nd year CPI | Version 1.0*

---

## What it does

- Records monthly **income** and **expenses**
- Sorts expenses into **categories** (Food, Transport, Rent, Health, Education)
- Calculates the **monthly balance** automatically
- Shows a **pie chart** of expenses by category
- Shows **OVER BUDGET** (red) or **WITHIN BUDGET** (green)

## What you need

- Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021 or Microsoft 365 (or a compatible program)
- The file `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx`
- No internet connection and no macros

## The three sheets

| Sheet | Use it to |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Read your balance, totals, chart and budget status (do not type here) |
| **Transactions** | Enter your income and expenses |
| **Categories** | See the categories and set a monthly budget for each |

## Quick start

1. Double-click `SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx`. If you see **Protected View**, click **Enable Editing**.
2. Open the **Categories** sheet and type your **Monthly Budget** for each category.
3. Open the **Transactions** sheet. Select the first empty row.
4. Enter the **Date**, a **Description**, choose the **Type** (Income or Expense) and, for an expense, choose a **Category** from the list.
5. Type the amount in the **Income** *or* **Expense** column and press **Enter**.
6. Open the **Dashboard** to see your updated balance and chart.
7. Press **Ctrl + S** to save.

## Good to know

- Choose categories from the list; do not type them.
- Do not type in the **Month** column or on the **Dashboard**: they are calculated.
- Only rows 4 to 50 are counted (47 transactions).
- Use one file per month, because the Dashboard adds up all rows.
- The red warning compares your **total** expenses with your **overall** budget, not each category.
